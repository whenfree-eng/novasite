How it works

01

It lives in your real channels. Your actual inbox, your actual diary, your card machine. Not another app your customers have to use.

02

Nothing goes out without you. Every reply, every message, every commitment waits in one approval inbox. You read the exact words, you say yes. Approving is one tap.

03

Every action has a receipt. When a reply is sent you can see the proof from the mail server itself. When nothing came back, it says so and chases at the right moment.

Your numbers say where they came from. A figure read from your bank or your diary is marked as measured. A guess is never dressed up as a fact. A part of your business that is not connected says so, rather than showing made up numbers.
