// Questions

Good to know.

Where are you based? +

We're at 4 Craven Court, off Atlantic Street in Altrincham (WA14 5DY), easy to reach from Manchester and the wider North West.

Can I just hire the room without production? +

Absolutely. Plenty of people book the space by the hour or the day, run their own session, and walk out with clean audio and video files the same day. We get you set up and check your levels before you record.

What do I need to bring? +

Just yourself and your guests. Mics, mixer, cameras and lighting are all here and ready. Bring notes or a rough running order if you like, and we can help you shape one before you record.

How fast do I get my clips? +

Our editing pipeline is built for turnaround. Clip packages usually land within days of the session, packaged and ready to post, and we agree the timeline when you book.

Do you post the content for me? +

On Premium we handle titles, thumbnails and scheduling so it goes out without you lifting a finger. If you'd rather post yourself, we deliver everything named and ready to go.

I've never recorded a podcast before. Is that fine? +

More than fine. A lot of our clients are first-timers. No performance, just conversations. We'll make the room feel easy and get the best out of you.
